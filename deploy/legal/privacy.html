Holofy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-19 · Status: Closed test (beta).

This is a plain-English notice for a free, closed test version of Holofy. It is not legal advice. Replace the contact email below with your real support address before sharing.

Who we are

Holofy ("we") is a trading-card scanner app, currently in a free closed test. We are an independent project and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, Game Freak, or The Pokémon Company. Card names and images belong to their respective owners.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Card captures: photos you take to scan, pre-grade, or check authenticity of your cards.
  • Your collection data: the cards you add, their condition, quantity, and the valuations we compute.
  • Basic technical data: a request identifier and standard server logs (no advertising trackers).

Why we use it

To identify and value your cards, run the pre-grade and authenticity checks you request, keep your vault and portfolio, and operate and secure the service.

Where it's stored

Your personal data is stored in the EU region. Card price data is sourced from public catalogues (e.g. TCGdex / Cardmarket) for display only.

Training data — opt-in only

We can improve our recognition and grading models using card captures, but only if you explicitly opt in. It is off by default. You can turn it on or off at any time in the app's Privacy screen. When you withdraw consent, we remove your captures from the training set.

Your rights

You can access your data in the app, withdraw training consent at any time, and permanently delete your account and all associated data from the app's Settings → Delete account. Deletion removes your account, captures, scans, collection, and portfolio history.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal data is erased. Anonymous, aggregate operational logs may be retained for a limited period for security.

Children

Holofy is not directed to children under 16. Do not use the test if you are under 16.
